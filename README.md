# BlueMoon Deck Analyzer & Tracker

A DIY companion client for BlueMoon that helps you visualize deck potential and track your collection in real-time. It provides a general idea of your deck's strengths across different gameplay phases based on your current build.

> **Disclaimer:** This is a community-made tool and is not 100% accurate. It is designed to offer a helpful overview rather than a perfect statistical guarantee.

## What It Is
* **A Companion Overlay**: A desktop client that runs alongside the game, providing a helpful tracker panel directly in your view.
* **A Strategic Analyzer**: A built-in dashboard that breaks down the math and phase distribution behind your deck.
* **A Collection Assistant**: A tool that syncs with your data to identify what you own and what you still need to acquire.

## What It Does
* **Phase Strength Distribution**: Categorizes your cards into Early, Mid, and Late game phases to show you where your deck is most powerful.

## How It Works
The app functions as a specialized browser for the game. When you open it, you can access the analyzer features to deep-dive into your collection or toggle the tracker while you play. The window is designed to intelligently resize so that your deck information never hides the actual game canvas.

To get started, you'll simply need to connect your game account within the app to let it fetch your latest decks and collection data.

## Disclaimer
This tool is a DIY helper. Strength values and phase classifications are estimates based on combat levels and metadata. Always trust your own gameplay experience over the analyzer's suggestions.
